// Features

Encrypted file sharing, built for control.

Every detail engineered so that your work stays yours — from the moment a file enters your vault to the second a client closes the tab.

End-to-end encryption

Files are encrypted in your browser before they ever leave your device. Not even we can read them.

Granular permissions

Decide who can view, download, or forward. Revoke access in one click, anywhere in the world.

Expiring links

Set links to self-destruct after a time window, a view count, or a single open. You choose.

Dynamic watermarking

Every viewer gets a unique forensic watermark baked into the preview. Screenshots never leave clean.

Real-time analytics

See opens, dwell time, and geography the moment it happens. Know which draft actually got attention.

Client portals

Give each client a branded space. No logins, no chaos, no endless email threads with attached zips.
